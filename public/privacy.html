Privacy Policy

APEX Humanoid · last updated 21 October 2026

APEX Humanoid ("the app", "we") is a procedural WebGL2 particle humanoid with an optional voice agent, gesture tracking and creative studio tools. This policy explains exactly what the app accesses, where data goes, and how to remove it.

1. Data we do not collect

  • No account is required, and there is no sign-up or login.
  • No analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers are bundled.
  • No personal profile is created or stored on our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with data brokers.

2. Device permissions and what they are used for

  • Camera — used only for on-device hand/gesture tracking so the humanoid's gaze can follow your hand. Frames are processed locally in the browser and are never uploaded or recorded. Camera use is opt-in; the app works fully without it.
  • Microphone — used only when you tap the mic button, for speech-to-text via the browser's Web Speech API. Audio is handled by your browser/platform speech service, not stored by us.
  • Local storage — small preferences (theme, gesture smoothing, saved companion settings) are kept in your browser's localStorage. This stays on your device and is cleared when you clear site data or uninstall the app.

3. Optional third-party services

The app has optional integrations that only activate when the operator configures an API key on the server. When your message is sent to a chat or voice feature, the text you typed may be forwarded to that provider to generate a reply:

  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech voice generation (/tts).
  • Google Gemini / OpenAI — conversational replies (/chat).
  • YouTube Data API — only if you connect your own Google account to upload a Shorts draft.
  • CoinDCX — only if the operator adds trading keys; without keys the trading bot runs in clearly-labelled simulated mode.

If no keys are configured (the default), the app uses on-device fallbacks and your text never leaves the server it is running on.

4. What the server receives

Standard technical data is processed to serve the app: your request path, a coarse timestamp, and whatever text you explicitly submit to a chat/voice endpoint. Server logs are kept only as long as needed to operate and debug the service.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Revoke camera/microphone at any time in Android Settings → Apps → APEX Humanoid → Permissions.
  • Clear local data via your browser/site settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • To request deletion of any server-side data associated with your usage, email us — we respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. API keys are held server-side and are never exposed to the browser. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes and material updates are noted in the app's release notes.
